A function-space characterization of RB-domains in Lean 4

Formalization case study

All content in this section is AI-generated. AI may make mistakes; please verify important information carefully.

This case study follows a function-space characterization of RB-domains from its mathematical manuscript into Lean 4. The formal development makes both the constructive core of the argument and the two external results on which the final theorem depends explicit.

Main result. For every domain X, the manuscript proves that X is an RB-domain if and only if the function space [X → E] is continuous for every domain E. More strongly, it constructs an algebraic domain AX such that continuity of the single function space [X → AX] already forces X to be an RB-domain.

The Luan–Li question

Miao, Luan, Liu, and Li proved that [X → E] is continuous whenever X is an RB-domain and E is a domain. Luan and Li then asked whether the converse holds and established it when X is algebraic. Their question appears in Function spaces of Lawson-compact algebraic domains. The present manuscript removes the algebraicity assumption from the source domain.

How the test domain works

The proof constructs a pointed algebraic domain EX from directed subsets and arbitrary subsets of X. Step functions into EX encode way-below requirements, while finite approximants below a canonical map can be converted into finite-image deflations of X.

To obtain a single decisive target, EX is paired with the three-element algebraic domain V having two incomparable minimal elements below a common top:

Test target. AX = EX × V.

The domain AX is not a disjoint union of pointed domains. Using the cited function-space dichotomy, continuity of [X → AX] therefore forces Scott compactness of X. A finite way-below cover can then be combined with the extraction construction to produce a directed family of deflations with supremum the identity.

What is formalized

The downloadable archive contains the public aggregate file and eight component modules: 9 Lean files and 1,857 lines in total. It formalizes the manuscript through thm:rb-characterization, including the test domains, requirements, finite-cover extraction, the function-space retract, and the final equivalence.

The principal public declarations are RBFunctionSpace.universalTarget, RBFunctionSpace.rb_implies_continuous_functionSpace, RBFunctionSpace.rbCharacterization, and RBFunctionSpace.singleTargetCharacterization. The later countably based uniform-test theorem and the manuscript's FS-domain section are intentionally not included in this formalized source boundary.

The final characterization is mechanically checked relative to exactly two project-local external axioms.
