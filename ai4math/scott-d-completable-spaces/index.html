Scott D-completable spaces

Cartesian closedness and a two-point test for maximality

How far can we extend the category of Scott dcpos without changing their function spaces? Monotone determined spaces give one extension, but they are not the largest. The paper shows that Scott D-completable spaces form a strictly larger Cartesian closed category. Among extensions that preserve the usual dcpo exponentials, it also identifies exactly where one must stop.

When a completion is a Scott space

Keimel and Lawson’s D-completion embeds a T0 space X into a monotone convergence space Xd. This completion is generated by the image of X under directed suprema and has a universal extension property. Let DX be its specialization dcpo. A space is Scott D-completable when the topology already on Xd is exactly the intrinsic Scott topology of DX. Write SDC for the full subcategory of these spaces, with ordinary continuous maps.

Products, function spaces and equalizers

The product in SDC uses the topology on X × Y induced by the Scott space of DX × DY. It need not have the ordinary product topology. For the exponential, extend each continuous map f: X → Y to a Scott-continuous map fd: DX → DY. Inside the pointwise ordered function dcpo, take the sub-dcpo generated by all these extensions. Its intrinsic Scott topology induces the topology on the original map set. With this topology, evaluation and currying give the exponential. Equalizers use the same idea: generate a sub-dcpo from the points where the maps agree, then use its intrinsic topology. These constructions make SDC finitely complete and Cartesian closed.

Why monotone determined spaces are not largest

Hui Kou conjectured that the monotone determined spaces form the largest full Cartesian closed subcategory of Top0 containing all c-spaces and all Scott spaces of posets. But every monotone determined space is Scott D-completable, and the inclusion is strict. A known example starts with countably many increasing columns whose suprema themselves form a chain up to a top point. Remove the column suprema but retain the top. In the resulting space, the singleton of the top is directed-open but not open; D-completion puts back the missing suprema and recovers the original Scott space. Thus SDC is a strictly larger Cartesian closed category containing Kou’s two classes.

Why the two-element chain suffices

Theorem 4.2. Let C be a full Cartesian closed subcategory of Top0 containing every Scott dcpo. Put T = {0 < 1}. If the canonical identification of the exponential (ΣR)ΣT in C with Σ[T → R] is a homeomorphism for every dcpo R with a bottom and a top, then C is contained in SDC.

The Scott space of T is the Sierpiński space. A Scott-continuous map from T into R is a comparable pair. Given a nonempty upper subset A of a dcpo P, the proof adds a new point a below exactly the points of A in P, together with a bottom and a top. The pairs (a, p) with p in A form a copy of ΣA in the new function space. The proof checks that this copy carries the intrinsic Scott topology of A, not just a topology inherited from ΣP.

Currying now shows that a continuous map into ΣP whose image lies in A remains continuous when its codomain is changed to ΣA. Apply this to an upper-set representation of the D-completion: the completion map is continuous into ΣDX, forcing X to lie in SDC. The paper also shows that the canonical exponential condition is equivalent to preservation of dcpo equalizers.

SDC itself preserves the usual dcpo exponentials, so it is the largest category under this compatibility condition. The paper leaves open whether there is a largest full Cartesian closed subcategory containing all Scott spaces and c-spaces without imposing compatibility.

Lean verification

The downloadable Lean project is for an earlier version. It proves Cartesian closedness, finite-limit results, the strict enlargement of monotone determined spaces, and largestness under canonical dcpo exponentials. The earlier proof uses a six-element test poset. The two-element test in the PDF is a stronger statement and is not covered by this ZIP.
