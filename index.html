Introduction

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the School of Mathematics, Sichuan University. My main research lies in domain theory and its connections with topology and the semantics of computation.

In domain theory, my work concerns powerdomains and powerspaces. These topics provide order-theoretic and topological tools for understanding approximation and nondeterministic or probabilistic computation.

I also work on artificial intelligence and machine learning. In parallel, I am interested in formalized mathematics, Lean 4, and AI-assisted theorem proving, especially methods that turn mathematical arguments into transparent and machine-checkable developments.
